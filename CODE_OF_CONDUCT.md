# Code of Conduct

**Last Updated:** December 2025  
**Version:** 2.0

---

## 🌟 Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a **safe, friendly, respectful, and welcoming environment** for everyone who contributes to **SRR(Smart-Resume-Reviewer)**.

This project is built for learning and collaboration.  
We expect all contributors to help create a **positive open-source community**.

---

## 🤝 Our Pledge

As contributors, maintainers, and community members, we pledge to:

- Treat everyone with respect and dignity
- Be kind and supportive, especially to beginners
- Encourage learning and growth
- Communicate clearly and politely
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Celebrate diverse perspectives and experiences

We welcome people of all:

- Experience levels (from complete beginners to experts)
- Backgrounds and cultures
- Genders and gender identities
- Nationalities and languages
- Skill sets and expertise areas
- Personal circumstances

---

## ✅ Expected Behavior

We expect all participants to demonstrate the following behaviors:

### Communication

- Use respectful and inclusive language
- Be patient with new contributors
- Provide helpful and constructive feedback
- Ask questions politely and answer them respectfully

### Collaboration

- Respect different opinions and ideas
- Focus on improving the project, not blaming individuals
- Celebrate others' contributions and successes
- Share knowledge and help others learn

### Examples of Good Behavior:

- ✅ "Thank you for your contribution! I noticed a small issue on line 42. Could you check it?"
- ✅ "Great idea! I have a suggestion that might improve this further..."
- ✅ "I'm new to this. Could someone help me understand how this works?"
- ✅ "I appreciate the feedback. Let me work on improving this."

### Resources

- Follow [GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines)
- Review our [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting PRs
- Read project documentation before asking questions

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are **not acceptable** in this project:

### Harassment & Discrimination

- Harassment, abuse, or threats of any kind
- Hate speech or discriminatory language based on race, gender, religion, nationality, etc.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Any form of intimidation or bullying

### Disruption & Misconduct

- Trolling, spamming, or deliberate disruption
- Publishing private information without permission (doxxing)
- Unsolicited sexual attention or inappropriate comments
- Persistent unwanted contact or stalking

### Examples of Unacceptable Behavior:

- ❌ "This code is terrible. You clearly don't know what you're doing."
- ❌ Using offensive slurs or discriminatory language
- ❌ Posting someone's personal information without consent
- ❌ Making inappropriate jokes or comments
- ❌ Dismissing someone's contribution because of their background

Such behavior will **not be tolerated** and will result in immediate action.

---

## 🔍 Code Review Guidelines

When reviewing code or contributions:

### Do:

- ✅ Provide constructive, specific feedback
- ✅ Explain the "why" behind suggestions
- ✅ Acknowledge good work and improvements
- ✅ Be patient with learning curves
- ✅ Offer to help or pair program if needed

### Don't:

- ❌ Make personal attacks or assumptions
- ❌ Use dismissive or condescending language
- ❌ Reject PRs without clear explanations
- ❌ Demand perfection from beginners

### Example Review Comments:

- ✅ "This is a good start! To make it more efficient, consider using [approach]. Here's why..."
- ❌ "This is wrong. Fix it." (Too vague and harsh)

---

## 🤝 Resolving Conflicts

If you encounter disagreements or conflicts:

1. **Stay Calm**: Take a step back and breathe
2. **Communicate Respectfully**: Focus on the issue, not the person
3. **Seek Understanding**: Ask clarifying questions before responding
4. **Find Common Ground**: Look for shared goals and values
5. **Escalate if Needed**: If resolution isn't possible, contact maintainers

### Conflict Resolution Process:

1. Try to resolve directly through respectful discussion
2. If unsuccessful, involve a maintainer as a mediator
3. Maintainers will review and provide guidance
4. Final decisions rest with project maintainers

---

## 🛠️ Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

### Core Duties

- Reviewing contributions fairly and promptly
- Providing respectful, constructive feedback
- Enforcing this Code of Conduct consistently
- Taking appropriate action when rules are violated
- Helping contributors feel safe, valued, and included

### Maintainer Rights

- Edit or remove comments, issues, or PRs that violate CoC
- Temporarily or permanently ban users who violate rules
- Make final decisions on project direction and contributions
- Delegate responsibilities to trusted community members

### Maintainer Commitments

- Respond to reports within 48 hours
- Handle all reports confidentially
- Provide clear explanations for any actions taken
- Continuously improve the project and community

## 🚨 Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

### Steps to Report:

1. **Stay Safe**: Do not respond aggressively or escalate the situation
2. **Document**: Collect relevant information:
   - Screenshots or links to problematic content
   - Timestamps and usernames
   - Context and background information
   - Any witnesses who can corroborate
3. **Report**: Contact project maintainers using one of these methods:
   - **Email**: [Add maintainer email here or create security@ email]
   - **GitHub**: Open a private issue with label `code-of-conduct`
   - **Direct Contact**: Reach out to maintainers via GitHub profile
   - **Security Issues**: Use [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/SharanyaAchanta/Smart-Resume-Reviewer/security/advisories)

### What Happens Next:

All reports will be:

- **Taken Seriously**: Every report is reviewed
- **Handled Confidentially**: Your privacy is protected
- **Reviewed Promptly**: Response within 48 hours
- **Handled Fairly**: Both sides will be heard
- **Resolved Respectfully**: Appropriate action will be taken

### Report Template:

```
Subject: Code of Conduct Violation Report

Description: [Brief description of the incident]
Location: [Link to issue/PR/comment]
Date/Time: [When it occurred]
Witnesses: [If any]
Additional Context: [Any other relevant information]
```

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

### Enforcement Actions:

1. **Warning**: First-time minor violations

   - Private message explaining the violation
   - Request to correct behavior
   - No public action taken

2. **Temporary Restriction**: Repeated or moderate violations

   - Temporary ban from commenting/contributing (1-30 days)
   - Required to acknowledge and commit to change
   - Monitoring period after return

3. **Permanent Removal**: Severe or persistent violations
   - Permanent ban from all project spaces
   - Removal of contributions (if necessary)
   - Public announcement (if appropriate)

### Enforcement Process:

1. **Report Received**: Maintainer acknowledges receipt
2. **Investigation**: Review evidence and gather information
3. **Decision**: Determine appropriate action
4. **Communication**: Inform all parties involved
5. **Follow-up**: Monitor and ensure resolution

The severity of the action depends on:

- Nature and frequency of the violation
- Impact on the community
- Whether it was intentional or accidental
- Previous history of violations
